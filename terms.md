## Part C. What a DroneMap result means

**C1. Purpose.** DroneMap compares your plan with a terrain model. Your plan is a takeoff point, an area or path, a flight altitude above takeoff, an RTH altitude and a margin. The app reports four things: terrain clearance, a safe RTH altitude, legal height above the ground, and terrain line of sight from the operator. Pro adds the Sun planner, file import and export, offline sites, larger areas and unlimited sites.

**C2. The ground data.** Heights come from a public bare-earth elevation model with samples about 30 m apart. Its vertical error can reach 16 m, and more on steep slopes, in narrow valleys and where the source had voids. Most of it was surveyed in 2000, so later changes to the land are missing. It holds **no buildings, trees, towers, wires, cranes or other obstacles**.

**C3. Built-in assumptions.** The RTH, altitude and line-of-sight results assume a typical drone. They assume heights measured from takeoff, an RTH that climbs and then flies home in a straight line, and a radio at 2.4 or 5.8 GHz. Your aircraft, its settings and its firmware may act differently.

**C4. The legal check.** The legal check applies the cap you choose (400 ft, 120 m or a custom value) to the terrain model. It is not legal advice. It knows nothing about airspace, waivers, exemptions or local permissions.

**C5. Sun windows.** The Sun planner uses geometry only. It ignores clouds, haze and objects around the site.

**C6. Things the app never does.** DroneMap does not connect to a drone. It shows no weather, no airspace and no no-fly zones. It does not authorize any flight.

**C7. You are the pilot.** Every result is an estimate and only a planning aid. **You carry full responsibility for each flight.** DroneMap must never be your only defense against a crash, a lost link, a lost aircraft or a rule violation. Keep visual contact with the drone and keep its own safety features enabled. Do not use DroneMap for crewed aviation, emergencies, evidence in legal matters, or any task where a wrong answer could cause injury, damage or loss.
